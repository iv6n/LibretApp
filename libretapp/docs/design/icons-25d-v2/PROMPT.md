# Prompt de la lámina modelo

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada (`image_gen`), sin CLI.
Salida: una lámina conceptual con seis ejemplos; no 64 recursos individuales.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE polished visual style-specimen board for a Spanish livestock management app, LibretApp, to establish a visibly dimensional but still simple 2.5D icon family. This is a single coherent concept sheet, not production assets. Create a beautiful landscape board with a precise 3-column by 2-row grid, exactly six large distinct icons, generous breathing room, warm nearly white ivory background (#FFFDF8), refined simple Spanish labels centered beneath each. Small heading at top left: 'LibretApp · Iconos 2.5D'. Secondary short line 'Modelo de estilo'. Icon labels in reading order EXACTLY: 'Registro', 'Agenda', 'Vacunación', 'Peso', 'Ordeña', 'Costo'. Objects in reading order: 1 Registro = cream notebook with green spine, three clean horizontal lines, a diagonal terracotta pencil; 2 Agenda = terracotta and cream calendar with two green binding rings, four large date squares and green check; 3 Vacunación = small golden medicine vial with cream label and medical cross plus one simple diagonal blue-green syringe; 4 Peso = green rounded weighing scale with cream dial and terracotta needle; 5 Ordeña = blue-green milk container with a prominent cream milk-drop relief; 6 Costo = cream receipt with 2 or 3 thick simple lines and one large golden coin with a single embossed minus sign. STYLE IS CRITICAL: premium crafted shallow bas-relief pictograms, front-facing nearly orthographic, just enough tilt to expose slim right and bottom sidewalls. Preserve graphic simplicity with clean broad silhouettes, rounded corners, 2 or 3 large parts, consistent stroke-free construction. Make real shallow depth unmistakable: individually designed beveled top edges, visible narrow darker side faces around each major part, inset panel details, subtle local occlusion shadows where components touch, thin controlled highlights following the actual edges. These are 2D drawings built from gently raised layers, with clear thickness, NOT just flat shapes with a global gradient and a drop shadow. Tactile matte satin surfaces; no photographic texture, no glitter, no glassy finish, no dramatic reflections. Same camera angle, same lighting upper left, same relief depth, same apparent size and weight across all six. Palettes limited to agricultural sage green #547663, dark green #345848, terracotta #C66B45, ochre #DAA54A, cream #FFF5DF, muted blue #7E9DA2 with coordinated light and shadow variants. Compact soft contact shadows directly beneath objects, no ambient scenes, no background circles or colored app tiles behind icons. Avoid tall extrusions, balloon-like clay toys, photorealistic 3D, isometric dioramas, overly detailed objects and tiny decoration. Each silhouette must still read well as a 24-pixel icon. Plenty of empty background between rows and columns, well-balanced margins, immaculate editorial presentation. No animals, no location illustrations, no watermarks, no extra labels. This is a NEW proposal with substantially clearer shallow relief than the previous barely shaded flat family.
```
