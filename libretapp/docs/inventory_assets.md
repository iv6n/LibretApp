# Inventory and Finance Image Assets

Inventory and finance illustrations use the shared 2.5D language: centered
square WEBPs with short gradients, soft contact shadows and integrated
highlights, warm agricultural colors, simple silhouettes, no text or
watermarks, and real RGBA transparency.

## Tanda 10 - Inventory and finance

- `assets/images/inventory_feed.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_medicine.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_tool.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_low_stock.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_income.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_expense.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_sale.webp`
- `assets/images/inventory_profitability.webp`

All Tanda 10 assets are compressed 256×256 WEBPs with real RGBA transparency.

The inventory list uses the specific assets for feed, medicine, tools and low
stock. If one of those files is unavailable at runtime, the corresponding
Material icon is shown as a fallback.

The finance dashboard uses the income, expense, sale and profitability assets
in its KPI cards and record lists, with the same Material fallback behavior.

## Naming convention

`assets/images/inventory_<concept>.webp`
