# Operational Record Image Assets

Operational record illustrations use the shared 2.5D language: centered square
WEBPs with short gradients, soft contact shadows and integrated highlights,
warm agricultural colors, simple silhouettes, no text or watermarks, and real
RGBA transparency.

## Tanda 11 - Operational records

- `assets/images/record_production.webp`
- `assets/images/record_movement.webp`
- `assets/images/record_cost.webp`
- `assets/images/record_sale.webp`
- `assets/images/record_reproduction.webp`
- `assets/images/record_service.webp`

All Tanda 11 assets are compressed 256×256 WEBPs with real RGBA transparency.

The production, movement, cost, commercial and reproduction forms display the
corresponding asset in their AppBar. If an asset is unavailable, the original
Material icon remains as a fallback.

## Naming convention

`assets/images/record_<record_type>.webp`
